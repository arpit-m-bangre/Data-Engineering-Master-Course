# 🌟 The 60 LPA Dream: Your Optimistic Roadmap

*The **DE 60 LPA PLAN.pdf** is not just a syllabus—it's a vision board for your future. Here is the optimistic, simplified breakdown of how you get there.*

---

### 🚀 Phase 1: The Launchpad (2025 - 2027)
**Goal:** Land your first high-paying role (18-24 LPA)
**Focus:** Build the ultimate foundation.
*   **Master the Big 3:** Python, Advanced SQL, and PySpark.
*   **The 200-Day Sprint:** Put in the daily hours (70% coding, 30% theory).
*   **The Result:** By Feb 2027, you will walk into interviews with 2 years of experience and the technical maturity of an engineer twice your age.

### 🦅 Phase 2: The Architect (2027 - 2029)
**Goal:** The 35-45 LPA Bracket
**Focus:** Scaling up and taking ownership.
*   **Master Orchestration:** Become an expert in Apache Airflow and dbt.
*   **Cloud Native:** Deeply understand AWS/Azure architecture and Snowflake.
*   **The Result:** You aren't just writing pipelines anymore; you are designing them from scratch and leading technical discussions.

### 💎 Phase 3: The Elite Tier (2029+)
**Goal:** The 60 LPA+ Dream
**Focus:** Solving impossible problems.
*   **System Design:** Architecting enterprise-scale platforms handling petabytes of data.
*   **Performance:** Optimizing cloud costs and Spark jobs at an extreme scale.
*   **The Result:** You are now a Senior Data Engineer at a FAANG or Tier-1 product company. You are paid not for the tools you know, but for the architectural decisions you make.
