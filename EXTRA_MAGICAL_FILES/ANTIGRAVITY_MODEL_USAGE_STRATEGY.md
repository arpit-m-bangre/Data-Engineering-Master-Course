# 🚀 Antigravity Free-Tier Survival Guide

Want to use the Antigravity IDE forever without ever hitting a limit or paying a cent? Just follow these **4 Super Simple Rules**!

---

## 🏓 1. The "Ping-Pong" Strategy (Balance your buckets!)
You actually have **TWO** separate fuel tanks. 
- 🔵 **Tank 1:** Gemini Models
- 🟣 **Tank 2:** Claude & GPT Models

**The Strategy:** Don't just drink from one tank! If you use Gemini all day, it will run empty.
- When Gemini drops to 50%, **switch to Claude**.
- While you are using Claude, your Gemini tank is secretly refilling in the background!
- **Example:** Think of it like letting your phone charge while you watch TV. By ping-ponging back and forth, you never have to wait!

---

## 🏎️ 2. Stop driving a Ferrari to the grocery store
You have access to different "sizes" of AI brains.

- 🧠 **Heavy Models (Pro / Thinking):** These are Ferraris. They burn massive amounts of fuel (quota). 
  - *When to use:* ONLY when you need me to rewrite 10 files at once or design a huge architectural project.
- ⚡ **Flash Models (Flash Medium):** These are your reliable daily Honda Civics. They use almost zero fuel and are lightning fast!
  - *When to use:* For 90% of your daily work! Asking quick questions, fixing a bug, or generating a single daily revision note.
  - **Example Model:** `Gemini 3.7 Flash Medium`

---

## 🛡️ 3. Keep the Safety Switch ON (Overages OFF)
In your settings menu, there is a toggle called **Enable AI Credit Overages**.
- **The Rule:** Keep this toggle **TURNED OFF**. 
- As long as this is off, the IDE can never accidentally charge you. If you ever hit 0% fuel across both tanks (which is super rare if you Ping-Pong), the IDE will just politely ask you to take a 2-hour break while the tanks refill for free.

---

## 🎯 4. The "One-Shot" Rule
Every time you hit *Enter* and send a message, it costs a little bit of fuel.
- **Bad Way:** Sending 10 short messages to fix tiny mistakes one by one. (Burns 10x fuel!)
- **Smart Way:** Giving me all your instructions perfectly in one big, clear message so I get it right on the first try.
- **Example:** Because we upgraded your `PROMPT.txt` and `AGENTS.md` files to automatically include Vocabulary and 2x Code Examples, you don't have to waste messages asking me to add them anymore! The system does it automatically in One-Shot!
