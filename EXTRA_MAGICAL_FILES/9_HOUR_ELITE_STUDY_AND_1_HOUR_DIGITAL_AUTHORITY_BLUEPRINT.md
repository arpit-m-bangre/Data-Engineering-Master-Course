## 🛡️ 4. THE 5 CORE RULES TO MAINTAIN 9.0 HOURS SUSTAINABLY

1. **The Context-Switching Firewall:**
   - Social distribution happens **only during the 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM block**. 
   - Once 11:30 AM hits, browser social tabs are closed, and SSMS becomes the only active window.
2. **Phone in Another Room:**
   - During Sprint 3 (Class Tasks) and Sprint 4 (14 Drills), your mobile phone stays physically outside your study room.
3. **The 3:30 PM Power Nap (Non-Negotiable):**
   - A 20–30 minute biological reboot between afternoon drills and project building restores prefrontal cortex glucose, keeping evening live class attention at 100%.
4. **Current-Focus Priority Protection:**
   - Morning sprints are strictly dedicated to yesterday's class note re-write and tasks. Today's live lecture is never crowded out by old debt.
5. **The 10:45 PM Hard Bedtime:**
   - To sustain 9 hours of high-energy cognitive output for 200 consecutive days, **7.5 hours of sleep is mandatory**. No coding past 10:30 PM.
